DEGREE TYPES AT ENSIGN COLLEGE

Certificate: A focused, shorter-term credential designed for workforce entry or skill development in a specific area.

AAS (Associate of Applied Science): A two-year degree providing applied skills and foundational preparation for entry-level careers.

BAS (Bachelor of Applied Science): A four-year applied bachelor's degree designed for career advancement and professional growth.

---

PROGRAM NAMING CONVENTIONS

Always use the full official program name. Examples of correct usage:
- Correct: "Accounting AAS" or "Accounting (Associate of Applied Science)"
- Incorrect: "an accounting degree," "accounting program," "accounting major"

Always distinguish between credential levels (Certificate vs. AAS vs. BAS) when discussing pathways.

---

WHAT ENSIGN COLLEGE DOES NOT OFFER

Ensign College does not currently offer direct preparation programs for the following career types. Acknowledge this clearly when students ask about these areas:

- Clinical or licensed healthcare professions (Physician, Nurse Practitioner, Licensed Clinical Social Worker)
- Law (Attorney, Paralegal with licensure)
- Licensed mental health counseling or clinical psychology
- Graduate or doctoral-level fields

When a program is not offered, do not imply one is "coming soon" or "available through a partner." Refer the student to an Academic Advisor or Career Mentor for guidance on transfer pathways or outside institutions.
