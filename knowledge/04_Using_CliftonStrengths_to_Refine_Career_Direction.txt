HOW CLIFTONSTRENGTHS FITS INTO CAREER EXPLORATION

CliftonStrengths helps you understand how you naturally think, work, and contribute.
It does not determine what career you should choose on its own.

At Ensign College, two assessments work together:

PathwayU: Helps identify what types of careers may fit you based on your interests, values, and personality.
CliftonStrengths: Helps refine how you might express those careers based on your natural ways of thinking and working.

The strongest career decisions consider both. Think of PathwayU as pointing toward directions, and CliftonStrengths as helping you understand how you would show up within those directions.

---

USING CLIFTONSTRENGTHS RESPONSIBLY

Use CliftonStrengths to:
- Clarify your preferred work style
- Identify environments where you are likely to thrive
- Reflect on roles that allow you to use your strengths regularly

Do not use CliftonStrengths to:
- Choose a career without considering other assessment data
- Rule out careers prematurely based on strengths alone
- Assume your current strengths limit your future growth

Strengths are not a ceiling. They are a starting point.

---

THE FOUR CLIFTONSTRENGTHS DOMAINS

CliftonStrengths organizes all 34 talent themes into four broad domains. Understanding your dominant domain helps clarify how you prefer to contribute at work.

EXECUTING

People with strong Executing themes often:
- Prefer structure and follow-through
- Gain satisfaction from seeing tangible results
- Thrive in operational or task-focused roles

Reflection question: "Which careers I have identified let me consistently complete, build, or improve something?"

Example fit areas: Operations, project coordination, accounting, logistics, process management

---

INFLUENCING

People with strong Influencing themes often:
- Enjoy persuading, presenting, or leading others
- Feel energized by visibility and direct interaction
- Thrive in roles centered on communication or advocacy

Reflection question: "Which careers allow me to communicate ideas or motivate others regularly?"

Example fit areas: Sales, marketing, public relations, leadership roles, content creation

---

RELATIONSHIP BUILDING

People with strong Relationship Building themes often:
- Value teamwork and deep collaboration
- Feel motivated by helping and supporting others
- Thrive in people-centered environments

Reflection question: "Which careers allow me to work closely with others and build trust over time?"

Example fit areas: Human resources, hospitality, education support, customer success, team coordination

---

STRATEGIC THINKING

People with strong Strategic Thinking themes often:
- Enjoy analysis, learning, and complex problem-solving
- Think about future possibilities and long-term implications
- Thrive in planning or conceptual roles

Reflection question: "Which careers allow me to analyze, design, or think deeply about challenging problems?"

Example fit areas: Business analysis, IT, cybersecurity, finance, research, strategy roles

---

COMBINING CLIFTONSTRENGTHS WITH PATHWAYU

Once you have both sets of results, ask yourself:

1. Which of my recommended PathwayU careers aligns best with my top CliftonStrengths domain?
2. Which work environments would let me use my strengths on a daily basis?
3. Where might I need to grow or develop new skills to succeed in a role I am interested in?

You do not need a perfect strengths-to-career match. Most successful professionals develop strengths that their roles require over time. The goal is alignment and awareness, not a perfect formula.

SELF-ASSESSMENT QUESTIONS TO EXPLORE YOUR STRENGTHS AT WORK

Consider which of the following feels most true for you:

On relationships at work:
- I want to build trust and collaborative teamwork. (Relationship Building)
- I want to network and build influence toward shared goals. (Influencing)
- I want structured, professional interactions that support productivity. (Executing)
- I value relationships when they help the team accomplish its mission. (Strategic Thinking)

On decision-making:
- I want input from others and want to feel supported before deciding. (Relationship Building)
- I want to find innovative approaches and challenge the usual route. (Influencing)
- I want to set aside emotion and evaluate options logically. (Strategic Thinking)
- I want to reach a decision efficiently and move forward. (Executing)

On what a great workday looks like:
- Team-building, shared wins, and knowing I helped the group succeed. (Relationship Building)
- Leading discussions, advocating for ideas, driving meaningful outcomes. (Influencing)
- Analyzing data, contributing to strategic decisions, generating insight. (Strategic Thinking)
- Completing projects, managing timelines, delivering measurable results. (Executing)

NEXT STEPS

- Use CliftonStrengths to refine - not replace - your PathwayU results.
- Share both sets of results with a Career Mentor to get the most out of both assessments.
- Remember that strengths grow through experience, effort, and intentional practice. Your top strengths today are a foundation, not a fixed identity.

To speak with a Career Mentor, visit the Career Services office or ask your advisor how to schedule an appointment.
