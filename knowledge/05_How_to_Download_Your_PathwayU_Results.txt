WHAT IS THE PATHWAYU CAREER EXPLORER REPORT?

PathwayU is an online career assessment platform used by Ensign College to help students discover career paths that align with their interests, values, work preferences, and personality.

After completing the assessments, PathwayU generates a Career Explorer Report - a personalized PDF that summarizes your results and suggests potential career matches. This report is the starting point for career coaching conversations at Ensign College.

---

HOW TO ACCESS AND DOWNLOAD YOUR CAREER EXPLORER RESULTS

Step 1: Log in to Career Explorer
- Go to ensign.pathwayu.com
- Sign in using your Ensign College student credentials

Step 2: Go to the Assessments page
- Navigate to the Assessments page (ensign.pathwayu.com/results)
- Confirm your four assessments (Interests, Values, Personality, Workplace Preferences) show completed checkmarks

Step 3: Locate the DOWNLOAD button
- At the top of the Assessments page, right next to the "PRINT" icon, click the "DOWNLOAD" button (with the download arrow icon: ⬇ DOWNLOAD)
- This downloads your official Career Explorer assessment results PDF to your computer or mobile device
- You can also click the black "EXPLORE CAREER MATCHES" button on the top right to see careers aligned with your profile

Step 4: Upload your PDF to this AI Coach
- Return to this coach and upload your downloaded PDF in Step 2 (or paste your results)
- NOTE: There is no separate "How to Download" link. Explain these steps directly to students in plain text!

---

SHARING YOUR RESULTS WITH THE AGENT OR A CAREER MENTOR

Once you have downloaded your report:
- You can share the PDF with this agent by uploading it to the conversation
- You can also bring the PDF to a Career Mentor appointment at Ensign College Career Services

The agent will be able to read your top career matches and begin connecting them to Ensign College programs and your CliftonStrengths themes (if available).

TROUBLESHOOTING

I cannot log in to PathwayU.
Contact Ensign College Career Services. They can resend your access invitation or help you reset your login.

My assessments show as complete but I cannot find the download button.
Try refreshing the page or logging out and back in. If the button still does not appear, contact Career Services for assistance.

I completed the assessments but my results look incomplete.
Make sure all four assessment sections are fully submitted, not just started. PathwayU requires all four sections to generate a complete Career Explorer Report.

I lost or deleted my PDF.
You can log back in to PathwayU and download it again at any time. Your results are saved to your account.

I can't access the Career & Major Exploration Roadmap / Ensign Connect.
Send an email to careerservices@ensign.edu or call 801-524-1925.

I do not have a PathwayU account yet.
PathwayU access is provided through Ensign College. Contact Career Services to receive your invitation link.
