# Case Study: Maria Chooses a Career and Major
Source Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WzWFwJpoLUE
Author: Rob Bagley, Ensign College Career Services

## 1. Context & The Student's Dilemma
Maria loves Ensign College, has made good friends, and is interested in many different academic subjects. However, as she looks at all the certificates and degrees Ensign College offers, she is overwhelmed and uncertain about how to choose her major and future career.

Maria discusses two contrasting approaches she sees among fellow students:
- Approach A (Jessica): Taking exploratory classes across completely different areas to see what she likes best. But Jessica worries this wastes time and tuition money, especially if she ends up disliking those courses.
- Approach B (Amari): Focusing strictly on finishing certificate classes first to get an immediate job that pays for the rest of his college education.
Maria does not know which approach she should take.

## 2. Parental & Spiritual Counsel
Maria calls her parents one evening. Her father gives key guidance:
1. Reflect on past enjoyment: Think about the things she has genuinely enjoyed doing and excelled at in the past.
2. Use campus resources: Seek out campus services rather than guessing alone.
3. Consult Career Mentors: Talk with Career Mentors to see how they can help.
4. Pray for divine guidance: Pray sincerely to receive spiritual confirmation and support.

## 3. Maria's Action Steps
1. Spiritual Foundation: Maria prays and studies her patriarchal blessing for insights on her talents and mission.
2. Faculty & Mentor Consultations: She meets with her teachers, academic advisors, and career mentors.
3. Brother Taylor's Recommendation: Her favorite instructor, Brother Taylor, advises her to visit Career Services and take the Career Explorer assessment.
4. Completing the 4 Assessments: Within one hour, Maria completes the 4 key assessments:
   - Interests (Holland Code)
   - Values
   - Personality (HEXACO)
   - Workplace Preferences

## 4. Resolution & Outcome
Through Career Explorer, Maria discovers exactly which academic subjects, certificates, and careers align with her unique strengths. She experiences immense relief knowing she is heading in a clear, purposeful direction that fits her interests and talents.
